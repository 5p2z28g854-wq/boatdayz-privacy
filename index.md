# BoatDayz – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

BoatDayz ("the App") helps you track and record your boating activities, including routes, distances, speed, duration, photos and trip notes.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal boating information under your control.

## 1. Information BoatDayz Uses

**Location Data**

BoatDayz uses your device's location to record boating routes, calculate distances and speeds, and support features such as arrival detection.

With your permission, location tracking may continue in the background while a BoatDay is active.

Your recorded routes and trip history are stored locally on your device.

**Photos and Trip Notes**

You may add photos and notes to your trips. These are stored locally on your device as part of your BoatDayz data.

**Weather Information**

BoatDayz uses Apple WeatherKit to display weather conditions and forecasts. Your approximate location may be processed by Apple to provide relevant weather information.

**Maps**

BoatDayz uses Apple Maps to display maps and boating routes. Apple's own privacy practices apply to its mapping services.

## 2. Data Storage

BoatDayz stores your boating records locally on your device.

BoatDayz does not operate its own cloud database or require you to create an account.

We do not operate advertising or analytics services, and we do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Sharing and Backups

BoatDayz allows you to export trips, create backups and share trip information using your device's sharing options.

You decide when to export or share this information and which recipients or services receive it.

Once information is shared with another person or third-party service, that recipient's privacy practices may apply.

## 4. Third-Party Apple Services

BoatDayz uses Apple services, including WeatherKit and Apple Maps.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

For more information, please review [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored BoatDayz information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App.

You can delete individual boating records using the available features in the App.

Exported files and backups may remain wherever you have saved or shared them and must be managed separately.

## 6. Children's Privacy

BoatDayz is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account, advertising or analytics system.

Location and other information entered into the App remain subject to the device permissions and sharing choices described above.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when BoatDayz features or privacy practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page.
